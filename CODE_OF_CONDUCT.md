# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

AI Watchdogs India (`aiwatchdogs.in`) is a strictly non-partisan, open-source civic-accountability community. We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free, evidence-driven, and legally responsible experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, caste, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, political views, religion, sexual identity and orientation, or socio-economic status.

## Civic-Audit Standards

Because our work examines public expenditure and official Government of India records, every contributor and node operator agrees to uphold higher evidentiary standards alongside general open-source courtesy:

1. **Evidence Over Accusation:** Discuss public records, statutory rules, and deterministic calculations — never engage in partisan invective, personal attacks, or unsubstantiated allegations of criminality.
2. **Respect Privacy & Indian Law:** Never upload, share, or paste personal identifying information (Aadhaar, PAN, private phone numbers, bank accounts, or private citizen names from welfare muster rolls) into issues, pull requests, findings, or swarm whispers.
3. **Zero Harassment or Doxxing:** Never target, dox, or harass public servants, journalists, vendors, or fellow contributors. Official oversight happens through lawful institutions (RTI Act 2005, CVC, PAC, CAG, CPGRAMS) and verified public documentation.
4. **Celebrate Cleared Records:** Valid refutations and cleared audit records are as valuable as verified anomalies. Treat peer disputes professionally and constructively.
5. **No Spam or Score Farming:** Do not manipulate contributor leaderboards with synthetic findings, duplicate nodes operated by the same person to bypass independent 3-of-3 peer verification quorum, or automated mass RTI filing.

## Expected Behavior

* Use welcoming, neutral, and inclusive language.
* Ground technical and audit disagreements in reproducible data, code, and statutory citations.
* Accept constructive peer review gracefully and update or retract leads promptly when official clarifications or mathematical corrections are presented.
* Respect the editorial independence of the swarm regardless of sponsorships or compute grants.

## Unacceptable Behavior

* Defamatory, hateful, communal, casteist, sexist, or abusive commentary.
* Submitting or encouraging CAPTCHA-bypassing scrapers, credentialed portal scraping, or denial-of-service traffic against `.gov.in` or `.nic.in` portals.
* Deliberately circumventing the PII scrubber (`scrub_pii`), the neutrality linter (`lint_neutrality`), or independent peer-verification quorum rules.
* Publishing private information of any individual without statutory basis and lawful redaction.
* Automated filing of RTI applications or vigilance complaints on behalf of humans.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, quarantine, or retract any finding, whisper, issue, or pull request that violates this Code of Conduct or [`LEGAL_AND_ETHICS.md`](./LEGAL_AND_ETHICS.md). Repeated or willful violations (such as deliberate doxxing, Sybil quorum manipulation, or abusive scraping) will result in contributor token revocation and permanent removal from the project.

To report a Code of Conduct concern, email **conduct@aiwatchdogs.in** (for security or PII incidents, see [`SECURITY.md`](./SECURITY.md)).
